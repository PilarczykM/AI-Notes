# Module 1: Introduction to Generative AI

## Topics Covered
- Introduction to Foundational Models and Terminology  
- Genealogy and Chronology of Large Language Models (LLMs) with Applications  
- Example Applications: Chat, Image Generation, Music Generation  
- Open-Source vs. Closed-Source Models  
- Prompt Engineering: Text and Image  

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this module, you will be able to:  
- Explain key concepts and terminology related to Generative AI and foundational models.  
- Understand the evolution of Large Language Models (LLMs), their genealogy, and real-world applications.  
- Compare and assess the differences between open-source and commercial Generative AI models.  
- Apply prompt engineering techniques in practical text-based and visual examples.  
- Identify and evaluate potential applications of Generative AI across various domains, including chatbots, image generation, and music.  

## Example Projects
- Crafting prompts for text-based AI models.  
- Designing queries for AI-powered image generation.  
- Selecting the appropriate model for specific tasks.  

This module provides a foundation for understanding and working with Generative AI, equipping you with the skills to explore its applications effectively. 🚀
